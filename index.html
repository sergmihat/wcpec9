The poster in three lines

MAE-Optimized Machine Learning Ensemble for Post-Processing GFS Solar Irradiance Forecasts in East Asia

  • Post-processing cuts the error of raw GFS by 11–21% at three BSRN stations (Ishigakijima, Tateno, Qiqihar; 2024, blocked 5-fold test).
  • Training on MAE instead of MSE lowers the MAE at every station; a sky-class bias correction keeps RMSE and bias level with MSE training.
  • With asymmetric imbalance prices, forecasting the cost-matched quantile instead of the mean cuts the cost by 18–22% at 3:1.
WCPEC-9, Daejeon, 15–20 November 2026
